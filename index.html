How the loyalty program works

  • Dine-in & Takeout only — this app is not for delivery orders.
  • Points and stamps are separate — points show top-right below your name; stamps live on the card above.
  • Earn 5 pts on every completed order. 1 pt = ₱1, good only toward drinks on a future order. No pts earned if you redeem points on that same order.
  • Earn 1 stamp per drink ordered (coffee, frappe, fruit shakes, milk tea) — after 9 stamps, your 10th is free. Your free pick is limited to any flavor of Large Milk Tea (22oz). 2 for ₱99 (M) earns just 1 stamp per order, not 2, and isn't eligible as the free pick.
